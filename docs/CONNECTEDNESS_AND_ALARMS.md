# Connectedness and living alarms — 23 September 2026

Model 0.8.0 replaces the fixed number of exposed regions with a main **Regional connectedness** dial. The previous region-count control is removed. Old saved worlds migrate `(reach − 1) × 20` to the new score and announce recalculation; this preserves the broad local-to-global intent, not the old outcome.

## How spread works

For score `c`, use `p = c / 100`. Region 1 is the initiating region. A named shared rollout draw and a named per-region adoption draw must both fall below `p` for another region to share the failing AI system. The common rollout draw correlates these failures.

Directed service links (1→2→3→4→5→6→2, plus 1→4) each have a named draw. A link transmits failure when its source is hit and its draw is below `p`. Iterate until no new region is reached. This handles cycles without counting damage twice. The graph and coefficients are explicit educational assumptions, not a measured world network.

Increasing connectedness with the same replay cannot remove a region from the footprint. All six footprint sizes are possible at the middle setting. At zero, only the initiating region is affected; at 100, all six share the failure. Other settings permit variation, including worldwide failure below 100. The dial is a connectivity score, not a stated probability of catastrophe.

Healthy donors send relief at their existing capacity multiplied by `0.25 + 0.75p`. Independent routes provide limited access at zero. Donor health, stable recovery, finite surplus and travel delays still apply. This creates a trade-off: stronger links spread damage and help carry relief, depending on who survives.

The sampled network remains fixed while changing controls. “Try another version of events” changes the replay, including regional links and human decisions. A 30-day replay uses one sampled network throughout; individual releases do not resample it. Footprints are applied at incident onset: the model does not calculate inter-region propagation times. Health/information background challenges retain the same footprint assumption; no epidemic dynamics are implied. These limitations are in About and on-demand rules.

## What the user sees

- An eighth main dial, with its own cyan identity and a live count of regions hit.
- A regional overview showing why each region is hit or spared: common AI system versus dependency on another region. Each entry opens that region's readout.
- Updated story, replay, month and About copy. Replay distributions now include worldwide disruption and civilisation crisis.
- Whole red housings cast broad breathing shadows; amber housings pulse more slowly; green housings hold a steady glow. Widespread crises use a stronger shared rhythm. Active red and amber wires carry travelling signals. Motion remains on by default with the existing pause control.

## Validation

93 tests pass. Added checks cover fixed-seed reproducibility, monotone exposure across the dial, correlated/shared and dependency routes, non-contiguous regional exposure, finite aid and main-dial-only collapse/recovery/protection. Existing two-survivor tests now explicitly use a sampled four-region network (score 65, seed 9), rather than assuming the first four regions are exposed at every seed.

[Reproducible range audit](CONNECTEDNESS_AUDIT.json), generated by `node scripts/audit-connectedness.mjs` after a build: at connectedness 50, 1,000 model replays span all six footprint sizes. With ordinary opening-world defences all 128 sampled worlds finish functioning at scores 30, 50 and 80; weakened repair crews, tool backup and food stores permit persistent disruption and collapse. No collapse threshold was loosened.

Main controls alone also show three endings at seed 42, connectedness 80: independent services 0 gives collapse; 10 gives recovery after collapse; 0 with 120-hour hospital backup avoids collapse. These are reproducible teaching scenarios, not forecasts.

Browser checks: dial endpoints give one/six regions; mixed exposure correctly leaves Region 5 working while Region 6 is hit; regional explanations distinguish both routes; 256-run worker results render; whole-housing animation computes intermediate glow values despite reduced-motion browser preferences. Undo restored the user's migrated settings after endpoint testing. No hosting or real-user test was performed.
